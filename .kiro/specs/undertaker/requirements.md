# Undertaker requirements

1. The Crew App shall register Package Graveyard and Repo Reaper scan types.
2. The Crew App shall run a selected scan immediately or on a schedule.
3. The Crew App shall persist scan results and timestamps locally.
4. The Crew App shall display evidence-backed findings grouped by severity.
5. The Crew App shall support approve and dismiss actions for revival plans.
6. The Crew App shall export approved plans.
7. The Crew App shall provide an offline fixture mode for judging.
